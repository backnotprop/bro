---
name: converge
description: Pull a sprawling session back into focus. Use when work has drifted across tangents, side research, or open threads and needs to be synthesized against the goal, or when the user says things like refocus, where are we, back on track, rabbit hole, land this.
disable-model-invocation: true
---

Step back from the session and converge.

Name the north star: the goal this work exists to serve. Infer it if it was never stated, and say so.

Distill what the exploration actually established. Then judge every open thread against the north star: keep what serves it, cut what doesn't, park what matters later, and say so plainly if what we learned should move the north star itself.

End with where we stand and the one thing to focus on next. Be shorter than the sprawl you're resolving.
